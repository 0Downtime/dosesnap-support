DoseSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

DoseSnap is a medication reminder and proof-photo app. It helps you create medicine reminders and save a photo record after taking a dose.

Information DoseSnap Stores

DoseSnap stores the information you enter in the app on your device, including:

  • Medicine names
  • Dose notes
  • Reminder times and schedules
  • Proof photos
  • Proof history dates and times

DoseSnap does not require an account and does not upload this information to a DoseSnap server.

Photos and Camera

DoseSnap asks for camera or photo access so you can save a proof photo after taking medicine. Proof photos are stored locally on your device. You can delete proof history from the app settings.

Notifications

DoseSnap asks for notification permission so it can alert you when it is time to take medicine. You can manage notification permission in iPhone Settings.

Data Sharing

DoseSnap does not sell, rent, or share your medicine reminder data or proof photos. If you choose to export or share a proof history PDF, that action is controlled by you through the iOS share sheet.

Deleting Data

You can delete proof history, reminders, or all app data from DoseSnap settings. Deleting the app from your device also removes the local app data stored by DoseSnap.

Medical Disclaimer

DoseSnap is a reminder and personal record tool. It is not medical advice and does not replace instructions from your doctor, pharmacist, or care team.
