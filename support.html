DoseSnap Support

Need help with DoseSnap? Start with these checks.

  • Open Settings in DoseSnap and use Send Test Notification.
  • Confirm notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings.
  • Confirm camera access is allowed if proof photos are not working.
  • Use View Tutorial Again in DoseSnap Settings to replay the first-time guide.
  • Use Export Proof PDF in DoseSnap Settings if you need to share proof history.

Safety Note

DoseSnap is a reminder and personal record tool. It is not medical advice and does not replace instructions from your doctor, pharmacist, or care team.
